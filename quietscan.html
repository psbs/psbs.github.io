Quiet Scan

A free document scanner for Android. Scan paper to PDF and share it, with no ads and no account.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Finds the page in the camera view and captures it automatically
  • Crops, straightens and cleans up each page, with Auto, Color, Grayscale and Black & white filters
  • Combines many pages into one PDF in A4, Letter or Auto size
  • Shares the PDF to any app, or saves a copy where you choose
  • Keeps unfinished scans as drafts

What it doesn't do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • No account, no subscription, no paid version, no watermark
  • No Internet permission: scanning and PDF creation happen on your phone
