Luma Couplet

Turn one mirror. Change two paths. A calm puzzle game about light.

Get it on Google Play

How it plays

  • Tap a mirror to turn it; mirrors ringed in the same colour turn together
  • Send every light to the target of its own shape, all at the same time
  • Cross both stars on the way for three stars
  • 68 original puzzles in six worlds, with decoy mirrors, snares and waypoints in the later ones
  • A hint when you want one, and undo as far back as you like
  • Soft original music and sounds, each with its own volume

What it doesn't have

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no account
  • No Internet permission: it plays offline and progress stays on the device
  • No timers, no lives
