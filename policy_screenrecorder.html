Screen Recorder by PuzzleRig privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · App package com.puzzlerig.screenrecorder

Screen Recorder by PuzzleRig records your phone's screen, lets you watch and edit (trim, crop, rotate, resize) the recordings, and saves them on your device. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it doesn't collect, send or share any.

Information the app collects

None. The app has no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking code. It doesn't have the Internet permission, so it can't send anything from the device.

Your recordings

Recordings are saved only on your device, in the Movies/Screen Recorder folder, where your gallery and other apps can also see them. Watching and editing happen entirely on the device. An edited video is saved as a new copy; the original is never changed. A recording leaves your device only if you share it yourself, using Android's share menu, and then the app you share it to handles it under its own policy.

Permissions and when they're used

  • Screen capture. Each time you start recording, Android asks you to allow sharing your screen (or one app). The app can't skip or remember this. You can stop at any time from the app, its notification, its Quick Settings tile, or Android's own controls.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO). Requested only when you choose an audio option. The microphone is recorded only if you pick an option that includes it. Android also requires this permission to record sound from other apps ("device audio"). Audio is saved only inside your recording.
  • Camera (CAMERA). Requested only when you turn on the camera bubble. The front camera is on only while a recording with the bubble is running, and turns off when you stop or tap "Hide camera". The bubble is a window on your screen, so your face appears only inside the screen recording; camera images are not saved or used anywhere else.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW). Requested only when you turn on the camera bubble, so it can float over the app you're recording. It's used for nothing else.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later). Used to show that a recording is in progress, with pause and stop buttons, and when a video has been saved. Recording works without it.
  • Foreground service for screen capture, microphone and camera. Android requires this so a recording keeps running, visibly, while you use other apps.

Information stored by the app

Besides your recordings, the app keeps its settings in its own private storage: video quality, frame rate, audio option, countdown, theme, and whether it has already asked for notification permission or shown the "notifications are off" tip. None of this identifies you, and the developer never receives it. It is removed when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. The app turns off Android's cloud backup for this data. Uninstalling the app doesn't delete your recordings, because they're stored as ordinary videos in your Movies folder.

Audience

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. It contains no advertising, purchases, chat or social features, and it collects no information from anyone, whatever their age.

Links

The only web link in the app is the one to this page, in Settings → Privacy policy. It opens your device's web browser. The app itself doesn't go online.

Sharing

The developer doesn't share data with anyone, because the app doesn't collect any.
