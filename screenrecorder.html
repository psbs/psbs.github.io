Screen Recorder by PuzzleRig

Record your screen. Edit it. Share it. Videos stay on your phone.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • One tap to start; record your whole screen, or one app on Android 14 and later
  • Sound from apps, your microphone, or both
  • Pause and resume; stop from the app, the notification or the Quick Settings tile
  • Auto quality up to 1080p, and 60 fps where your phone supports it
  • Optional camera bubble (circle, rounded or square) you can drag and resize while you record
  • Watch recordings in the app; trim, crop, rotate or shrink them, always saved as a new copy
  • Saved as MP4 in Movies/Screen Recorder, ready to share

What it doesn't have

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no account
  • No Internet permission: recordings leave your phone only when you share them
  • No analytics or tracking
