Word Search Adventure privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · App package com.puzzlerig.wordsearch

Word Search Adventure by PuzzleRig is a word search puzzle game. You find hidden words in letter grids, read what each word means, and travel through five worlds of puzzles. This policy explains what the game does with information. The short version: it doesn't collect, send or share any.

Information the game collects

None. The game has no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking code. It doesn't have the Internet permission, so it can't send anything from the device.

Permissions

The game uses the Android VIBRATE permission for optional game vibrations, which you can turn off in Settings. Optional local reminders are off by default. POST_NOTIFICATIONS is requested only when enabling reminders; RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED allows Android to retain their schedule after restart. Personal performance, league, calendar and fictional cup records remain on this device. It has no Internet permission. AndroidX also declares an internal, signature-protected receiver permission; this does not grant access to personal information.

Information stored by the game

To remember your progress, the game keeps a small amount of information in its own private storage on your device:

  • which puzzles you have solved, and which of them you solved without a hint;
  • the words you have found (your Word Collection);
  • stars, best scores, badges, daily puzzle completion dates and the reward ledger;
  • earned coins, cosmetic purchases and your selected board, word and sparkle styles; coins cannot be bought with money;
  • for a puzzle you haven't finished: the words found so far, hints used, misses and consecutive finds;
  • the puzzle you played last, and whether the game has been opened before;
  • your settings: Sound, the Music and Sounds volumes, Vibration, Bigger letters and Calm motion.

None of this identifies you, and the developer never receives it. The game turns off Android's cloud backup for it, so it stays on this device only. It is removed when you choose "Start again from level 1" in Settings (which keeps your settings), when you clear the game's data, or when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It contains no advertising, purchases, chat, social features or links to other apps, and it collects no information from anyone, whatever their age.

Links

The game contains no web links. This policy is also shown in full inside the game, in Settings → About and privacy.

Sharing

The developer doesn't share data with anyone, because the game doesn't collect any.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Any future version that changed what the game collects would say so here before release.
