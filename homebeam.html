Homebeam

The fleet is stranded. Light will bring it home.

Get it on Google Play

How it plays

  • Tap a mirror to turn it; mirrors with the same coloured frame turn together
  • Each ship needs light of its own colour before it can launch
  • Keep every beam out of the black holes, and pass light through the ships along the way
  • 68 missions across six regions of the star map
  • Cross both stars on the way for three stars, with a hint when you want one
  • Original music and sounds, each with its own volume

What it doesn't have

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no account
  • No Internet permission: it plays offline and progress stays on the device
  • No timers, no lives
