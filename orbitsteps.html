Orbit Steps

Plan the path. Program the robot. A calm puzzle game on floating tiles in space.

Get it on Google Play

How it plays

  • Build a short program from Forward, Left, Right and Jump, then press Run
  • Collect the crystals and reach the flag; three stars for the shortest program
  • 30 handcrafted puzzles in three worlds: First steps, Warp pads and Repeat
  • Stuck? Pip gives a hint based on your own program
  • New robots, colours and badges to earn

What it doesn't have

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no account
  • No Internet permission: it plays offline and progress stays on the device
  • No timers, no lives
