Arrow Buzz: an arrow puzzle game for Android

Tap an arrow and it escapes along its path, but only if the way ahead is clear. Find the right order, free every arrow from the maze and clear the board.

Get it on Google Play

How to play this arrow escape puzzle

  • Find an arrow with a free path to the edge and tap it away.
  • Each arrow that leaves opens a path for the others.
  • Tap a blocked arrow and you lose a life, so think first.
  • Stuck? Use a hint, a hammer or another booster.

What's in the game

  • 180 levels across eight episodes, each checked to be solvable by reasoning alone.
  • 25 Grand puzzles bigger than your screen, up to 30 by 60, to explore by zooming.
  • Frozen arrows and locked arrows with numbered keys in later episodes.
  • Three lives (five on a Grand puzzle) and a countdown on each board. Every arrow you free adds time.
  • A daily puzzle, gift calendar, daily spin, quests and 32 hidden pictures.
  • Every puzzle plays offline.

Questions

Does it have ads? Only optional rewarded videos, which play when you choose one. Nothing is for sale.

Which language is the game in? English.

Which devices? Android phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or newer.
