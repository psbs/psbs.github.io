Calm Arrows privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026 · PuzzleRig Studio · App package com.puzzlerig.arrows

Calm Arrows is a puzzle game that you can play offline. It offers optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob. This policy explains what that means.

Our own data collection

None. The game has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own. It does not sell any data, and we receive no personal information about you.

Rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video for an extra heart, a revive, a hint, a second spin, double coins, free coins or a bigger daily gift. A video plays only when you tap a button that says so. However, Google's Mobile Ads SDK starts when the game opens and may load a video ahead of time, whether or not you ever watch one, subject to the consent choices below. Google's SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with the ads;
  • diagnostic information.

Google uses this to deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game shows Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Hearts and rewards → Privacy choices for ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. If you never watch a video, the game still works; videos simply are not offered while you are offline.

Permissions

The app uses the network, network state and advertising ID permissions, plus the AdServices and background-work permissions that the ads library adds. It does not ask for storage, contacts, location, camera, microphone or notifications.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, coins, hearts, boosters, rewards and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. "Start over" in Settings clears puzzle progress (coins and boosters are kept).

Android backup

If you have Android's own backup or phone-to-phone transfer turned on, Android may include this small save file so a new phone keeps your progress. Android handles this under your Google account settings.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Coins are earned in the game and cannot be bought or exchanged for money.

Children

The game is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Links

The only link in the game is the one to this page, in About. It opens your web browser.
